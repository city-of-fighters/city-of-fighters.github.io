How to connect (do this once per tournament)
  1. Log in on start.gg (a normal free account is fine) and create your tournament with one event, for example “Tekken 8”.
  2. In that event, add a bracket of the same type as here (Double elimination, Single elimination, Round robin or Swiss).
  3. Get an API key (once, from the account that runs the City of Fighters hub): start.gg → your profile picture → Developer Settings → Personal Access Tokens → Create new token. Copy it. It is like a password: never post it anywhere else. Paste it below and press Connect: from then on the Events calendar fills with your upcoming start.gg tournaments by itself.
  4. Open your event on start.gg and copy the link from the address bar (it looks like start.gg/tournament/…/event/…).
  5. Paste the link and the key below and press Connect. Players here with the same gamer tag are linked automatically.
  6. Players register on start.gg with their start.gg profile (the app's Register tab sends them there). They appear here by themselves within 3 minutes, with start.gg's seeding, so both brackets match. Get players from start.gg does it right away.
  7. Start the bracket here, and press Start on the bracket in start.gg too.
  8. That's it: every result you enter here (control panel, phone or Stream tab) goes to start.gg within a few seconds while this control panel or the Stream tab is open on a computer. Undo here resets it on start.gg.

    📲 Get the apps (computer and phone)

    Computer: the control panel and Stream tab in their own window. It always shows the latest version.

    Windows installer (.exe) Windows portable (.exe, no install) Linux (AppImage)

    Windows may say “Windows protected your PC” because the app is not signed: click More info → Run anyway. On Linux, make the AppImage executable (right-click → Properties → Allow executing) and open it.

    Phone: open the app on your phone and tap Get the app: choose your phone, then your browser, and follow the steps.
