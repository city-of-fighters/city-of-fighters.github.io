Taking part

  • Use a gamer tag that isn't offensive and isn't someone else's. Organizers may rename or remove registrations that break this, are duplicates or are fake.
  • Your gamer tag and results are shown publicly, including on the Twitch stream and in recordings of it.
  • Organizers decide match results, disputes and disqualifications. Their decision is final.
  • Matches may be streamed. If you don't want to be on stream, tell an organizer before your match.
