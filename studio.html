How to connect to OBS (once, takes 2 minutes)
  1. In OBS, open the scene you stream with (the one with your game capture).
  2. Under Sources press + and choose Browser. Name it City of Fighters and press OK.
  3. In URL paste this link:
  4. Set Width 1920 and Height 1080. Leave Custom CSS as it is (it keeps the background see-through).
  5. Tick Refresh browser when scene becomes active, then press OK.
  6. In the Sources list, drag City of Fighters above your game capture so it sits on top. If it does not fill the screen, right-click it → Transform → Fit to screen.
  7. Done. Everything you Deploy below appears on stream within 2 seconds. If OBS ever looks stuck, right-click the source → Refresh.

Only this one source is needed: scoreboard, popups, bracket, standings, winner and podium all come through it. Nothing is shown to viewers until you deploy.
