City of Fighters · start.gg login

What I Need From You

I’m connecting “Log in with start.gg” to the City of Fighters app, so players can log in with their own start.gg accounts. Your account runs the City of Fighters hub, so this part has to be done from your account. It takes about 5 minutes.

What you need to send me

Client IDFrom the app you create in the steps below.
Client SecretFrom the same page. Treat it like a password.

Steps

  1. Log in to start.gg with the account that runs the City of Fighters hub.
  2. Click your profile picture, then Developer Settings. Or open start.gg/admin/profile/developer.
  3. Open OAuth Applications and click Create Application. The names may be slightly different.
  4. Name it City of Fighters.
  5. Set the redirect URL to exactly this, with nothing added or missing:

    https://cityoffighters.com/events.html

  6. Save it. Copy the Client ID and the Client Secret.
  7. Send both to me privately.

How to send them safely

  • Send them in a private message to me only, or tell me in person.
  • Don’t post them in a group chat, a public place or a screenshot.
  • Once I confirm I have them, delete the message.

don’t send me your start.gg password. I don’t need it, and players’ passwords are never seen by the app either.

What this does

  • Players see a Log in with start.gg button in the app.
  • The app only reads the player’s public start.gg profile, such as their gamer tag.
  • It doesn’t change anything on your hub or tournaments.
  • The app is in test mode, so it sends no results or sign-ups to start.gg.

If you get stuck

If you can’t find the page, or it asks for something not listed here, tell me what’s on your screen. Don’t send the secret in a screenshot.

If the secret is ever shared by mistake, make a new one on the same page and tell me.
