Phone App Manual

This manual explains how to install and use the City of Fighters app on a phone. The first part is for everyone. The last part, Admin, is for organizers.

1. Install the app

Open https://cityoffighters.com/app.html on your phone and tap Get the app. If you closed that box, the Get the app link is at the bottom of the page. Choose your phone, then follow the steps it shows.

Android (Android 7 or newer)

  1. Tap Android, then Download the app (easiest), then Download the app.
  2. If your phone warns you about the file, tap Download anyway.
  3. Open the file from your notifications or your Downloads folder.
  4. If your phone asks, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, and go back.
  5. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  6. Open City of Fighters from your home screen.

The warnings appear because the app isn’t from Google Play yet. If you’d rather not use the file, choose your browser in the list and follow those steps instead.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up). If you can’t see it, tap ••• first.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open City of Fighters from your home screen.

First time you open it

The app asks whether it may save your login and small settings on your phone. Choose Allow saving or Don’t save anything. The app works either way; without saving, you log in again each time.

Updates

The app updates itself because it shows the live website. Close it and open it again to get the newest version.

2. Leaderboard

The first button at the bottom. Anyone can use it.

  • Watch it live on Twitch opens the City of Fighters Twitch channel.
  • Now on stream shows the match being streamed, with its round and live score.
  • Standings lists each player’s place, wins and losses (or points), and games won and lost.
  • Bracket shows the tournament. Tap a round to see its matches. In round robin and Swiss tournaments this button is called Matches.
  • Past tournaments, at the bottom, lists finished tournaments. Tap one to see its final standings and every result.

When a tournament ends, a gold banner shows the champion. If no tournament is running, the page says so.

3. Events

The second button. It lists upcoming City of Fighters events by month, with the date, time, game and place. Times show in your phone’s own time zone.

  • Sign up on start.gg opens that event’s page on start.gg.
  • More info opens a link the organizers added.

4. Register

The third button. Players register only with a start.gg account.

  1. If you don’t have a start.gg account, create a free one at start.gg.
  2. Tap Register in the app, then the button for the tournament or event.
  3. Sign up on the start.gg page that opens.

Your name then appears in the app within a few minutes. This depends on the organizers having linked the tournament and having the Control panel or Stream tab open on a computer.

Player titles

On the Leaderboard, tap Player titles to see all the titles and what they mean. Once Log in with start.gg is switched on, a logged-in player can choose up to 5 there and press Save my 5 titles. Titles such as Champion or GOAT can only be given by organizers.

5. Admin: for organizers

The fourth button is your remote control during a tournament. It needs the admin password.

  • 3 wrong passwords lock the login for 15 minutes, even for the right password.
  • You stay logged in for 24 hours. Log out is at the top.
  • Tournaments are created on a computer, not on the phone.

See what OBS shows

At the top of the Score and OBS tabs is a live picture of OBS, updated every second. The Stream tab must be open and connected on the PC. If it isn’t, a message says so.

Score tab

Shows the match on stream, or the one you picked under Matches.

  1. Each player has a big button. When a player wins a round, tap their button once.
  2. The app counts for you: 3 rounds win a game, and 2 games win the match.
  3. When the match ends, the app moves the players on in the bracket.

Matches tab

Lists every match that can be played now.

ScoreScore that match round by round.
On streamPuts that match on the stream.
2–0 and 2–1 buttonsEnter the final score of a match played elsewhere, in one tap. Choose the row for the winner.

Bracket tab

The same bracket viewers see. Tap a round to see its matches.

OBS tab

Choose what viewers see right now: Scoreboard, Bracket, Standings or Podium. It changes on the stream right away. The podium also appears by itself when the tournament ends.

6. If something goes wrong

The app looks oldClose it completely and open it again.
“Too many wrong passwords”Wait 15 minutes. Devices already logged in keep working.
Wrong player tappedTap Undo.
A finished match is wrongOn a computer: Control panel → Finished matches → Reopen.
No live OBS pictureOpen the Stream tab on the PC and connect to OBS.
The Android file won’t installCheck you have Android 7 or newer and allowed installs from your browser. Or use the browser install.
A browser bar shows in the Android appTell the organizers. The app should open full screen.
