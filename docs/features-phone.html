Every feature · 6 November 2026

The phone app

Everything the phone app can do. Anyone can use the first three tabs. Admin is for organizers and needs the password. Features marked “needs setup” switch on after the steps in the “Connect email and start.gg” PDF.

The app has 4 buttons at the bottom: 🏆 Leaderboard, 📅 Events, ✍️ Register and 🔒 Admin.

Getting the app

📲 Get the appChoose your phone (Android or iPhone/iPad), then your browser, and see only the steps for that combination. Copy link button for other browsers.
Download the app (Android)The Android app file (APK), from the site. Needs Android 7 or newer. The phone may ask you to allow the install.
Install nowOn Android Chrome, a one-tap install button when the browser offers it.
Find it againA 📲 Get the app link at the bottom of the Leaderboard, if you pressed Not now.
UpdatesThe app updates itself. Close it and open it again for the newest version.
Privacy choiceThe first visit asks whether the app may save your login and small settings. Allow saving or Don’t save anything; the app works either way.
Privacy and termsLinks at the bottom of the pages.

🏆 Leaderboard (everyone)

● Watch it live on TwitchOpens the City of Fighters Twitch channel.
Tournament nameThe tournament, game and type at the top.
🔴 Now on streamThe match on stream with its round and live score.
📊 StandingsPlaces, wins and losses (or points), and games won and lost.
🏆 BracketTap a round to see its matches: who won, who’s out and where they go next. In round robin and Swiss this is 🗓️ Matches.
ChampionA gold banner with the winner when the tournament ends.
Before it startsThe date, how many have signed up and a Register on start.gg button. If nothing is running, it says so.
🏛️ Past tournamentsEvery finished tournament with date, players and champion. Tap one for its final standings and all results.

📅 Events (everyone)

CalendarUpcoming City of Fighters events by month: date, time, game and place.
Your own timeDates and times show in the phone’s own time zone.
Sign up on start.ggA button on events with a start.gg link. Other events can have a More info link.
Events from start.gg (needs setup)Tournaments City of Fighters hosts on start.gg appear by themselves. Tournaments the account only plays in are left out.
Log in with start.gg (needs setup)Log in once and stay logged in (90 days from your last visit). Your start.gg password is never seen. Log out any time.
Participate (needs setup)One button on City of Fighters start.gg events. It becomes ✓ You’re in. If start.gg asks, its own page opens so you can confirm.

✍️ Register (everyone)

Register on start.ggThe only way to sign up. Opens the start.gg page for the current tournament.
No linked tournamentShows upcoming start.gg events with Sign up buttons, and All events on start.gg.
Log in with start.gg (needs setup)Replaces the buttons above: log in here, then join events from the calendar.
Into the bracketSigned-up players come in by themselves within 3 minutes, under their start.gg name, while a tournament is linked and the Control panel or Stream tab is open on a computer.

🔒 Admin (organizers)

Your remote control during a tournament. Log in with the admin password. 3 wrong passwords lock the login for 15 minutes. You stay logged in for 24 hours; Log out is at the top. Tournaments are created on the PC.

Live view of OBS

At the top of the Score and OBS tabs: what OBS is showing, with a new picture every second. The Stream tab must be open and connected on the PC.

🎮 Score

Big player buttonsTap the player who won the round. The name, games and round dots are on the button.
Automatic counting3 rounds win a game, 2 games win the match.
− round, + game, − gameFix a score by hand.
↶ UndoSteps back through your last actions on this phone.
ON STREAM / OFF STREAMShows whether viewers can see the match you’re scoring.
📺 Put this match on streamPuts an off-stream match on stream.

📋 Matches

Ready matchesEvery match that can be played now.
🎮 ScoreScore that match round by round.
📺 On streamPuts that match on stream.
2–0 and 2–1 buttonsEnter the final score of a match played elsewhere with one tap.

🏆 Bracket

The same bracket viewers see, round by round.

📺 OBS

Choose what viewers see right now: Scoreboard, Bracket, Standings or Podium.
