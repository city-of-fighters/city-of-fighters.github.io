Every feature · 6 November 2026

The PC app

Everything the PC app can do. It’s for organizers and needs the admin password. It shows the live website in its own window, so it updates itself. Features marked “needs setup” switch on after the steps in the “Connect email and start.gg” PDF.

The app window

Ctrl + 1Control panel: tournaments, players, players, results.
Ctrl + 2Players: add, edit, photos.
Ctrl + 3Stream: what OBS shows, cameras, Coming Up.
Ctrl + 4Sponsors: add or delete logos.
Ctrl + 5Settings: start.gg, calendar, test email, past tournaments, downloads.
View menuReload (Ctrl + R), full screen and zoom.
No internetShows a message and tries again every 5 seconds.
LinksOther websites open in your normal browser.
Login3 wrong passwords lock it for 15 minutes. You stay logged in for 24 hours. Log out is in the Tournament box.

Control panel: tournament

📲 Get the appsDownload links for Windows (installer or portable) and Linux, and how to get the phone app.
＋ Create a new tournament on start.ggOpens start.gg, where tournaments are made. They appear in the calendar by themselves.
Start tournament on the day (needs setup)A start.gg event shows at the top on its day. One click creates the tournament, brings in the players and makes the bracket with start.gg’s type. A finished tournament is kept under Past tournaments first.
Tournament typesDouble elimination, single elimination, round robin (points) or Swiss (points).
SettingsChange the name, game, date and rules, then Save settings.
Reset bracketDeletes the bracket and results, keeps the players.
End tournamentThe app then says no tournament is in progress.
Players boxThe seeding list with ▲ ▼, Shuffle seeds, team names and Remove. Start tournament here makes the bracket again after Reset bracket.
Ready to playEvery match that can be played now, with Put on stream.
On streamThe current match, with Take off stream.
StandingsLive places.
BracketThe full bracket.
Finished matchesAll results, with Reopen to undo a finished match.
↶ Undo last actionSteps back through your recent actions on this computer.
Results imageWhen a tournament ends, a picture of the results with Download PNG.
📧 Email the winner banner (needs setup)The champion gets a banner by email once, automatically, or when you press it. The player needs an email in the Players tab.
Past tournamentsEvery finished tournament is saved by itself, with Delete for each one.

Settings tab

Things you set once or rarely. Test mode is on by default: the app reads from start.gg but sends nothing to it, until you untick it.

Connect (needs setup)Save the start.gg API key once. Start tournament links each event by itself; a link can also be pasted by hand. Instructions are in the box.
Get players from start.gg (needs setup)Adds signed-up players with their team and seeding. It also runs by itself every 3 minutes until the bracket starts.
Send results automatically (needs setup)Every result goes to start.gg within seconds, with the game score, while the Control panel or Stream tab is open. Tick or untick it.
Send results now (needs setup)Sends waiting results straight away.
Undo on start.gg (needs setup)Undoing a result here resets it on start.gg too.
Problems list (needs setup)Says in plain words why a result couldn’t be sent.
DisconnectRemoves the link. Results already sent stay on start.gg.
Events calendarAdd, edit and delete events: name, date, game, place and link.
Events from start.gg (needs setup)Hosted tournaments appear by themselves every 30 minutes. ↻ Get events from start.gg now does it at once.

Players tab

Add a playerGamer tag, plus optional team and email. Useful for someone without a start.gg account.
EditChange anyone’s tag, team, email or photo, even after the bracket has started.
PhotoUpload a picture, then drag and zoom to choose what the square shows. Remove photo takes it away.
No photoShows just the name, never an empty box.
RemoveRemoves a player before the bracket starts.

Sponsors tab

Add a sponsorType the name and pick a logo (PNG, JPEG or WebP). A PNG with a see-through background looks best. It’s resized automatically.
DeleteRemoves a sponsor from the list and the stream.
On stream by itselfOBS updates within about 10 seconds. Nothing to refresh.
Where it showsBottom right, one logo at a time. Up to 30 sponsors.

Choose a layoutScoreboard, Bracket, Standings or Podium.
Match on the scoreboardPick which match shows.
PreviewSee it before viewers do. Preview Coming Up shows that screen too.

Stream tab: OBS control

Connects by itselfThe PC app reads the OBS password from OBS’s own settings. In a normal browser you type it once.
＋ Add cameraAdds the camera and picks one that really gives a picture, skipping laptop infrared sensors.
Camera listPick the device, switch In the stream / Hidden, or Remove.
Coming Up sceneChoose the game and cameras, then Set up Coming Up scene in OBS.
One camera, two playersCuts one camera in half, one half per player. Swap the halves turns it around.
Cameras during matchesSquare outlined boxes in the top corners, under the character pictures.
Scene switchingOBS goes to Coming Up between matches and back when a match goes on stream.
Phone live viewSends a small picture of OBS each second, only while a phone is watching.

What the stream shows

ScoreboardTop of the screen: names, teams, photos, games won and the round.
LogosGaming Malta and City of Fighters bottom left. Sponsors bottom right, fading in one at a time for 4 seconds each.
Result popupsOff-stream results fade in for about 5 seconds, then fade out.
Winner screenThe winner and photo for 6 seconds, for a result entered in the app or on start.gg.
Player cardsFull-screen cards of both players for 4 seconds at the first black screen after Put on stream, when both have a picture.
Switch sidesSwaps the two players on stream. The bracket doesn’t change.
Go live / End streamStart or stop the Twitch stream, for admins. The Stream tab must be open and connected to OBS.
Coming UpThe game in the big window, cameras labelled PLAYER 1 and PLAYER 2, and the next round on top.
Bracket and StandingsThe whole tournament, animated as players move on.
PodiumTop three with photos, crown and confetti. Appears by itself after the last winner screen.
