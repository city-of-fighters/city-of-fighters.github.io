OBS Manual

This manual explains how to connect City of Fighters to OBS, use it on stream, and update the PC app.

1. Install OBS

  1. Download OBS from obsproject.com/download and install it.
  2. Open it. If it offers an auto-configuration wizard, choose optimizing for streaming.

2. Turn on OBS remote control

  1. In OBS, open Tools → WebSocket Server Settings.
  2. Tick Enable WebSocket server. Leave authentication on. Click OK.

This lets the Stream tab control cameras and scenes.

3. Add the stream page

Do this once. It’s the only source you need for the scoreboard, popups, bracket, standings, winner screen and podium.

  1. Click the scene you stream with. Under Sources, click + and choose Browser.
  2. Name it City of Fighters and click OK.
  3. In URL, paste https://cityoffighters.com/overlay.html
  4. Set Width to 1920 and Height to 1080. Click OK.
  5. In the Sources list, drag it to the top. Top of the list means on top of the picture.
  6. If it doesn’t fill the screen: right-click it → Transform → Fit to screen.

already have it with the old github.io link? Double-click the source and change the URL to the one above.

4. Add the game

With a capture card

  1. Plug in the capture card and turn on the console.
  2. Under Sources, click + → Video Capture Device, and pick the card.
  3. For sound: + → Audio Input Capture, and pick the card.

From this PC’s screen

Under Sources, click + → Screen Capture (PipeWire) on Linux, or Display Capture on Windows. Pick the screen.

Keep the game below City of Fighters in the Sources list.

5. Connect Twitch

  1. In OBS, open Settings → Stream.
  2. Choose Twitch, click Connect Account and log in. Click OK.

6. Connect the Stream tab

  1. Open OBS first, then the Stream tab (Ctrl + 3).
  2. In the PC app, it connects by itself and shows “Connected to OBS”.
  3. In a browser: in OBS open Tools → WebSocket Server Settings → Show Connect Info. Copy the password into the Stream tab and press Connect.

Once connected, the Stream tab has Go live and End stream buttons under Twitch stream. They start and stop streaming in OBS, and each asks you to confirm first. Admins also have them in the Control panel and the phone’s OBS tab. Those only work while the Stream tab is open and connected on the OBS computer.

7. Cameras and the Coming Up screen

Cameras

  • Add camera adds the camera on this PC. It picks one that gives a picture.
  • The list next to each camera changes the device.
  • In the stream / Hidden shows or hides it.
  • Remove takes it out of OBS. The game keeps running.

Coming Up screen

Between matches, OBS switches to a screen with the game in the big window and the player cameras in the two small ones. It switches back when the next match goes on stream.

  1. Choose the Game screen, Player 1 camera and Player 2 camera.
  2. One camera filming both players? Tick One camera films both players. Its left half goes to Player 1 and its right half to Player 2. Tick Swap the halves to turn that around.
  3. Keep Also show the player cameras in the bottom corners during a match ticked if you want those.
  4. Press Set up Coming Up scene in OBS. Do this again after changing cameras or the game source.

don’t delete the OBS scenes “CoF Coming Up”, “CoF Players camera” and “CoF HUD cameras”.

8. Use it on stream

  1. In the Stream tab, click Scoreboard, Bracket, Standings or Podium.
  2. Pick the match for the scoreboard.
  3. Look at the Preview. Viewers can’t see it yet.
  4. Press Deploy to OBS. It’s live within 2 seconds. Now on OBS shows what’s live.
ScoreboardTop of the screen: names, teams, photos, games won and the round.
Result popupsOff-stream results fade in for about 5 seconds.
Winner screenShows for 6 seconds after a match on stream.
Player cardsBoth players of the next match, mirrored, with portraits and 5 titles. Pick it in the Stream tab or the phone’s OBS tab. When both players have a picture, the cards also show for 4 seconds at the first black screen after the match goes on stream (right after the stage is chosen). This needs the Stream tab connected to OBS with the Game screen chosen; otherwise they show on Put on stream.
Coming UpBetween matches, with cameras labelled PLAYER 1 and PLAYER 2.
PodiumTop three with photos. Appears by itself at the end.
LogosGaming Malta and City of Fighters bottom left; sponsors bottom right.

To test: pick Bracket and press Deploy to OBS. It should appear in OBS within 2 seconds. Then pick Scoreboard again.

The overlay is see-through, so with nothing to show you see only the game.

9. Update the PC app

Pages inside the app update by themselves; press Ctrl + R to reload. You only need a new install for app changes, such as new menu items or shortcuts.

Windows

  1. Close the app.
  2. Open Settings → Get the apps and download the Windows installer.
  3. Open the file. If Windows warns you, click More info, then Run anyway.
  4. Install it over the old one. You don’t need to uninstall first. Your login and settings stay.

Linux

  1. Close the app.
  2. Download the Linux (AppImage) file from Settings → Get the apps.
  3. Replace your old copy. If you’re unsure how it was installed, ask whoever set up the PC.

10. If something goes wrong

“Could not reach OBS”Open OBS, and check section 2.
OBS shows old things or nothingRight-click the City of Fighters source → Refresh cache of current page.
Overlay doesn’t fill the screenRight-click it → Transform → Fit to screen.
The game covers the overlayDrag City of Fighters above the game in Sources.
A camera window is blackPick the other device in the camera list. Still black? Close and reopen OBS.
Coming Up shows the wrong thingsPress Set up Coming Up scene in OBS again.
Phone shows no live pictureOpen the Stream tab on the PC and connect.
